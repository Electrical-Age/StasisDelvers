# The Workbench Ledger of Arthur Vance

*Substation 4, High Moor. Kept for the Kid, or for whoever pried the door open.*

## How to read this book

Every subject in this ledger comes in two halves. The **Diary** is what I built, day by day, as the stillness came up
the valley: what went wrong, and the physics behind it in plain English. Read it first, and in order, if you don't
know a volt from a volume of water. The **Reference** is my tidied-up notes: tables, ratings, which port goes where,
and what the gauges mean. Read that when something is already whistling at you. The Diary links to the Reference
wherever a number or a rule comes up, and every Reference page links back to the Diary entry that explains it.
